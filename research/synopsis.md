# Synopsis: information-matched resolution reliability audit

Develop an International Journal of Remote Sensing research article that
audits error and uncertainty scores under spatial-resolution shift in disaster
imagery. The study must explain and quantify the gap between reference-dependent
multi-scale consistency scores, which compare a degraded image with unavailable
finer views, and deployable scores that use only the received image. It must
include exact-hash-deduplicated AIDER training, at least three independent model
seeds, matched-information confidence/KNN/ViM/consistency baselines, measured-GSD
evaluation on leakage-free CRASAR spatial splits, paired UAS/satellite sites
from at least two disaster events, spatial-cluster uncertainty, negative-result
preservation, and complete executable provenance. The intended contribution is
an audit protocol and evidence-backed deployment guidance, not an unsupported
claim that a failed gate is operationally superior.
